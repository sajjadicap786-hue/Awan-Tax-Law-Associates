Awan Tax Law Associates provides income tax return filing and related compliance support for individuals and businesses in Pakistan. Our services cover salaried returns, business and freelance income, rental income, NTN registration, wealth statements, ATL status reviews and assistance with FBR notices.

Our office is based in Satellite Town, Rawalpindi. Our service focus extends throughout Pakistan through online communication and an agreed document-sharing process. Whether you live in a major city or another part of the country, the first step is the same: tell us your situation so we can understand the support you need.

Start with the facts

Every client has a different combination of income, assets and records. We ask about those circumstances before discussing the work. This helps us establish a relevant checklist and explain which information is needed for preparation.

Make the process understandable

We aim to explain the prepared figures and the information behind them. Questions are part of the process. You should have the opportunity to review personal details, income records and statements before approving submission assistance.

Agree on the scope

Fees and timelines depend on the work involved and the completeness of your documents. We discuss the scope and service fee before proceeding. If further information is needed, we explain the gap and how it affects the next step.

Keep useful records

Organised records help during preparation and later enquiries. We encourage clients to retain acknowledgements, income certificates, deduction records and the documents used in their filing. Our initial enquiry does not require uploading confidential documents to this website.
