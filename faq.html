FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your tax questions.
Clearly explained.

Practical answers before you start. For guidance on your own circumstances, speak with us directly.

Discuss your requirements ↗
Can overseas Pakistanis arrange Pakistan tax filing online?

Yes. Tell us your country of residence, relevant tax year, Pakistan income sources and filing history. We discuss the documents and scope before preparation. Read our detailed overseas Pakistani service guide.

Does living abroad automatically decide my Pakistan filing position?

No. Your circumstances, residence position, income sources and the relevant tax year need review. We do not apply a blanket conclusion to every overseas client.

Can you assist clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work online with clients worldwide to prepare and file Pakistan income tax returns, including overseas Pakistanis. You can complete the process remotely without visiting our office. Contact us by WhatsApp or phone to discuss your requirements and agree on a private document-sharing channel.

Can you help with a first-time return?

Yes. We first review your registration status and income sources, then explain the records needed for preparation.

What if I have salary and other income?

Tell us about all income sources, including business, freelance work and rent. The document checklist should reflect your full circumstances.

How are fees decided?

The fee depends on income sources, record complexity and the work required. We discuss the service scope and fee before beginning.

How long does preparation take?

The timeline depends on complete documents and the complexity of the work. We discuss an expected timeline after reviewing your situation.

Do I need to visit the office?

You can begin online. If an office visit is helpful, call first to arrange it at Office No. 216, Saleem Heights, Satellite Town, Rawalpindi.

Can I send documents through the website?

This website does not collect documents. The enquiry form opens a prepared WhatsApp message. Agree on a private document-sharing channel before sending records.

Can you guarantee a refund or a particular tax rate?

No single outcome applies to everyone. Tax treatment and any refund position need to be reviewed against your circumstances, records and the relevant tax year.

What should I do if I receive an FBR notice?

Keep the complete notice and note the stated response date. Contact us to discuss a review of the notice and the supporting information requested.

Will I review my return before filing?

Yes. We explain the prepared figures and ask for your review and approval before assisting with submission.

Which documents should I keep afterwards?

Keep the return acknowledgement, statements and supporting income, deduction and transaction records used in preparation.

Where should I check current deadlines and announcements?

Use the official FBR website for current announcements. Deadlines and requirements can change; ask us to review the information relevant to your tax year.

What if some documents are missing?

Tell us which records are unavailable and explain any uncertain figures. We discuss how the information gap affects preparation and what supporting information might be needed. An incomplete record should not be treated as a confirmed amount.

Can you review more than one income source?

Yes. Describe all the sources at the beginning, including salary, business, freelance work and rent. We discuss the documents needed for each source and the scope of the combined preparation.

What if my circumstances have changed since my previous return?

Mention changes in employment, business activity, ownership, borrowing or major transactions. Keep the records explaining these changes so they can be considered during preparation.
