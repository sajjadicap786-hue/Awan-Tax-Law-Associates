UNDERSTANDING THE SERVICE

Support built around
your situation.

A tax return should reflect your actual income and supporting records. We help bring salary, business, professional and other relevant income details together in one organised preparation process. Rather than starting with a form, we start with your circumstances: the tax year, your income sources, your previous filings and any changes during the year.

What we review

Income from more than one source needs careful organisation. A salary certificate alone may not describe your full position if you also receive rent, freelance receipts or business income. We review the available information, identify missing records and discuss the next steps before preparation.

How we work with you

Begin by contacting us on WhatsApp or by phone. Tell us the relevant tax year and the type of assistance you need. We discuss your records, identify information gaps and explain the proposed work and fee. Documents are shared through an agreed private channel. Please avoid sending passwords or one-time codes in your first enquiry.

A prepared return is shared for your review. You should check the income amounts, personal particulars and underlying information before approving submission. After filing, keep the acknowledgement and supporting records together so they are available when you need them.

Available throughout Pakistan

You can arrange assistance online from anywhere in Pakistan. Our office is based in Satellite Town, Rawalpindi, but you do not need to visit in person to start the conversation. We keep the next steps clear and explain what is needed from you as preparation progresses.

What happens after the review?

Depending on the agreed scope, the next step may be preparation, additional document collection or an explanation of the matters that need attention. We do not submit a prepared return without your review and approval. The available acknowledgement and records should be kept for future reference.
