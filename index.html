BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A useful starting checklist

The exact documents depend on your income and tax year.

  • CNIC and taxpayer registration details
  • Salary certificate or income records
  • Bank statements and tax deduction certificates
  • Business, rental or freelance income records
  • Details of assets, liabilities and expenses
  • Previous return, if available

Please do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive documents through a public comment.

MORE THAN A FORM

Understand your records.
Make your next step clearer.

Tax filing begins with your actual circumstances. We help you bring the information together and understand the preparation process.

Support for different income sources

Salaried professionals, shopkeepers, business owners, freelancers and landlords may need different starting documents. If you receive income from more than one source, tell us about each one. We discuss the relevant records together so the preparation reflects your circumstances rather than a generic checklist.

For salaries, gather income and deduction certificates. For business or freelance receipts, organise invoices, payment records and relevant expenses. For rental income, keep tenancy details and a record of payments. Your exact checklist is discussed after the initial conversation.

Preparation you can follow

We explain the work, ask for relevant documents and identify information gaps before moving forward. You have an opportunity to review the prepared details and ask questions. Clear records and prompt clarification help make the process easier for both first-time filers and clients returning for another year.

The scope and fee are discussed before work begins. We do not advertise one outcome for every taxpayer or use an unsupported figure to fill a missing record. The aim is an organised process based on the information you provide.

A nationwide way to get started

Our office is in Rawalpindi, but online assistance is available throughout Pakistan. You can start from Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta or another city by contacting us on WhatsApp. Tell us which service you need and the tax year you want to discuss.

There is no document upload on this website. The enquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp, where you review and send it. Once the work is understood, we agree on a private channel for sharing the relevant documents.

YOUR QUESTIONS, EXPLAINED

A little clarity
before you start.

Have a different question? Tell us what you need help with.

Can you help me with my first tax return?

Yes. We begin by understanding your income sources and registration status, then explain the information needed to prepare your return.

Can I get assistance online?

Online assistance is available. We agree on an appropriate communication method and document-sharing process before preparation begins.

How much does return filing cost?

The fee depends on your income sources, record complexity and work required. We discuss the scope and fee before starting.

How long will the process take?

Timing depends on complete documents and the complexity of your return. We discuss the expected timeline after an initial review.

Will you explain the return before submission?

We explain the prepared figures and request your review and approval before assisting with submission.

Where can I check official tax information?

For official announcements and taxpayer resources, visit the Federal Board of Revenue website. We can help you understand how the relevant requirements relate to your situation.
