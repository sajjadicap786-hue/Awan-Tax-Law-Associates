UNDERSTANDING THE SERVICE

Support built around
your situation.

An FBR notice should be read in full before deciding how to respond. We help review the document, identify the information being requested and organise relevant supporting records. Share the complete notice through an agreed private channel, including attachments or references that form part of it.

What we review

The wording of a notice, the tax year and the stated response date all matter. Please do not rely on a short screenshot of only one paragraph. An initial review helps us understand the scope of assistance and whether additional information is needed.

How we work with you

Begin by contacting us on WhatsApp or by phone. Tell us the relevant tax year and the type of assistance you need. We discuss your records, identify information gaps and explain the proposed work and fee. Documents are shared through an agreed private channel. Please avoid sending passwords or one-time codes in your first enquiry.

Response preparation is based on the facts and documents available. We discuss the proposed work and service fee before proceeding. Do not ignore a notice or assume that an earlier return submission answers every request for information.

Available throughout Pakistan

You can arrange assistance online from anywhere in Pakistan. Our office is based in Satellite Town, Rawalpindi, but you do not need to visit in person to start the conversation. We keep the next steps clear and explain what is needed from you as preparation progresses.

What happens after the review?

Depending on the agreed scope, the next step may be preparation, additional document collection or an explanation of the matters that need attention. We do not submit a prepared return without your review and approval. The available acknowledgement and records should be kept for future reference.

Read the full request before responding

Keep every page of the notice, including references, attachments and stated dates. Gather the relevant return and earlier correspondence so the sequence of events can be understood. Identify the particular question being asked rather than sending a general collection of unrelated documents. If you have already replied, provide that reply and its acknowledgement. This helps establish what information has been supplied and what may still need attention.

Prepare an evidence-based explanation

A useful response should be organised around the matters raised and the records supporting the explanation. We discuss which documents relate to each point and where further information is needed. Do not alter records to match an assumed answer or send an incomplete document without explaining what is missing. The work and fee are agreed after an initial review. The scope of assistance depends on the notice and your circumstances, and no outcome is promised before that review.

Before you contact us

Write down the tax year, your income sources and the specific question you want help with. Mention any previous filing and changes in your circumstances. A concise overview makes the first conversation more useful. You do not need to send a complete set of confidential documents just to ask about the service. Once we understand the work, we explain the starting checklist and agree on a suitable way to share records.

Review, approval and follow-up

Preparation depends on information that is complete and understandable. Please tell us when a figure is uncertain or a document is unavailable. Where the agreed work produces a prepared return or statement, review the details and ask questions before approving submission assistance. Keep the available acknowledgement and supporting records afterwards. If new information becomes available later, contact us to discuss what it changes and whether a separate review is needed.

A clear scope for your engagement

Our initial discussion identifies the work you need, the information available and the proposed service fee. A simple task and a return involving several income sources may require different levels of preparation. Timelines also depend on receiving the relevant records and resolving questions promptly. We explain the agreed steps so you know what to provide and what happens next. Contact us on WhatsApp at +92 333 5232992 to begin.
