MATCH YOUR NEED TO THE SERVICE

What do you need
help with today?

I want to file a return

Choose income tax return preparation, or the overseas Pakistani service if you live abroad. Tell us about every income source and the relevant tax year. We identify the documents needed for your circumstances.

I need registration or status support

Choose NTN registration for profile-related help, or ATL review for a status and filing-history enquiry. Tell us whether an existing profile or acknowledgement is available.

I have a statement or transaction question

Choose wealth statement, rental income or freelance support according to the records involved. Explain substantial transactions or receipts you do not understand so they can be reviewed.

I received a notice

Choose FBR notice assistance. Retain the complete notice and its stated dates. The initial review establishes the information requested and the proposed scope of response preparation.

Fees and work are discussed before we begin

The service cards describe areas of support, not identical packages for every client. The income types, available documents and questions involved determine the work required. We explain the proposed scope, fee and preparation steps before proceeding. If several services are needed together, mention them at the beginning.
